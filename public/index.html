About the Property

Tranquil Estate Stay in Coorg.

Anudina Kuteera is an estate homestay in Madikeri, Coorg, set in the tranquil surroundings of coffee and arecanut plantations with a serene lake-facing view. The property features a well-maintained four-room villa that can be reserved as individual rooms, by floor, or as an entire private stay, making it suitable for families, couples, groups, and extended visits.

Guests can enjoy amenities such as a swimming pool, open garden space, shared dining area, reliable WiFi, and dedicated bonfire and barbecue areas. For those interested in local experiences, guided fishing and an off-road safari in Coorg can also be arranged, with the safari being especially enjoyable during the monsoon season. Anudina Kuteera is managed with a focus on comfort, hospitality, and an authentic Coorg homestay experience. Check-in at 2 pm and check-out at 11 am.
